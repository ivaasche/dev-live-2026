Transform Your Retail Business with AI That Delivers Real Results 

In today's competitive landscape, AI experimentation isn't enough. While 80-85% of retailers are testing AI solutions, only 15-20% have successfully scaled them across their business. Those who do? They're seeing 3x ROI and 30% higher financial valuation metrics. 

Join retail transformation leaders from Google Cloud and Valtech for an exclusive, insight-packed roundtable where we'll cut through the AI noise and provide clear, actionable strategies to accelerate your AI adoption journey. 

What You'll Discover: 

Beyond Proof-of-Concept: Breaking the AI Implementation Barrier 

Learn why retailers get stuck in PoC mode and how to overcome common obstacles to full-scale AI deployment. We'll share Google Cloud's proven adoption framework that's helping retailers move from experimentation to organization-wide implementation. 

AI That Drives Revenue: Cutting-Edge Use Cases & Real Results 

Explore how leading retailers are implementing transformative AI solutions: 

  • Revolutionary in-store digital experiences that blend physical and digital shopping 
  • Next-generation AI concierge services creating personalized customer journeys 
  • Breakthrough generative enterprise search capabilities that transform discovery 
  • Content generation that scales personalization across all customer touchpoints 

The Strategic AI Roadmap: Building for Long-Term Success 

Discover how retail leaders are structuring their AI implementation for sustainable growth, proper governance, and maximum business impact. 

Why Attend: 

  • Network with retail innovation leaders facing similar challenges 
  • Gain exclusive insights from Google Cloud and Valtech retail specialists 
  • Leave with actionable strategies you can implement immediately 
  • Enjoy refreshments and valuable networking opportunities 

Limited Seats Available 

Reserve your place at this exclusive roundtable for retail decision-makers. Join us to discover how your organization can be among the 20% of retailers successfully scaling AI and capturing significant competitive advantage.
