Content is produced across teams, tools and channels, but it rarely connects. Product data sits in one system, assets in another. Workflows are manual. AI is layered on top without fixing the foundation.

The result: more content, more complexity, less impact.

The Valtech Content Supply Chain identifies where content is breaking down and defines how to connect data, content, workflows and AI into a scalable system that drives measurable business outcomes.

Content only scales when it operates as a system, not a series of disconnected activities.

What we focus on

We target the orchestration points where content must deliver real impact:

  • Scale into a connected, enterprise-wide content system

FAQs

What is a content supply chain?

It’s the system that connects product data, content creation, workflows, and distribution so content can scale across channels and perform consistently.

How is this different from content production?

This isn’t about producing more content. It’s about fixing how content is created, managed and delivered across the business.

What problem does this solve?

It solves how content, product data and workflows connect and scale across your business, not just how content is produced.

Do we need a PIM, DAM or CMS in place?

No. Valtech works with your existing systems. We help connect and optimize them as part of a scalable model.

How does AI fit into this?

AI accelerates content creation and optimization, but only when it’s embedded into structured workflows and governed properly.

How quickly can we see value?

We focus on proving impact in weeks through targeted use cases before scaling across the organization.
