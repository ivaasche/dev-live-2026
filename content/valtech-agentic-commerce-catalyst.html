FAQ

What is included in the Valtech Agentic Commerce Catalyst?

The Catalyst identifies high-impact opportunities across your customer experience, assesses your readiness, and delivers a clear, prioritized roadmap to turn AI into measurable business outcomes.

How does the Valtech Agentic Commerce Catalyst enhance existing tools?

The Catalyst identifies where AI can unlock more value from your current search, personalization, and experience stack by focusing on the moments that drive conversion and customer decisions.

Do you need an existing AI strategy to benefit from the Catalyst?

No. The Catalyst is designed for organizations at any stage. It helps define where to focus, which opportunities to prioritize, and how to move forward based on your current environment.

How does the Catalyst help move from AI pilots to real results?

The Catalyst focuses on the experience moments where AI will deliver the most impact and defines a clear path to scale, turning isolated pilots into structured, outcome-driven initiatives.

Can the Catalyst work if your data is not fully ready?

Yes. The Catalyst works with your current data landscape. It identifies what can be used today, what needs improvement, and how to structure your data to support AI-driven experiences.

Is the Catalyst relevant if you are not ready for advanced AI or agents?

Yes. The Catalyst focuses on immediate, high-impact opportunities while helping you build the foundation needed to support more advanced capabilities over time.
