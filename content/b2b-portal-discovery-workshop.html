B2B portal discovery workshop

Transform your B2B portal into a high-performing revenue and customer-experience engine

Is your B2B portal delivering on its promise?

Today’s B2B buyers, distributors, partners and internal teams expect fast, intuitive self-service portal experiences. But many organizations struggle with low adoption, limited personalization, weak integrations or portals that function more like transactional tools than customer-engagement engines.

Our B2B portal discovery workshop helps you identify what it will take to turn your portal into a true driver of growth, loyalty and operational efficiency.

Why B2B portal performance matters

Ecommerce portals are becoming the primary growth engine for B2B:

  • 56% of all U.S. B2B revenue in 2025 will come from digital channels — up from 32% in 2020
  • 85% of sales teams’ administrative work can be eliminated through effective customer portals
  • 66% of B2B organizations are increasing their investment in customer portals in the next 12–18 months

However, most B2B portals still fail to meet expectations:

  • 70% of distributors say their online commerce initiatives are “unsuccessful”
  • 80% of B2B ecommerce portal projects underperform on key KPIs such as adoption, workflow automation and revenue contribution
  • 35% of B2B portals remain “closed” experiences with limited integration

What this workshop includes:

A 3–4 hour interactive session designed to help B2B organizations modernize, scale or optimize their portal experience.

  • Assess where your portal sits today and map the user journeys that drive or block adoption
  • Evaluate the strength of your ERP, CRM, PIM and middleware integrations to uncover data bottlenecks limiting automation and personalization.
  • Analyze current vs. target sales through the portal to uncover opportunities for growth, efficiency and cost-to-serve reduction.
  • Assess AI search, predictive recommendations, smart bundles and emerging AI use cases to improve discoverability and customer experience.
  • We identify where your organization sits relative to peers and highlight where to focus next.

What you'll receive

A custom action plan outlining:

  • Portal adoption accelerators
  • Integration & data improvement priorities
  • UX and workflow enhancements
  • AI, search and personalization opportunities
  • Revenue and efficiency levers
  • A 12–18 month prioritized roadmap

Who should attend

This workshop is ideal for teams responsible for your portal’s performance, including:

  • Digital and ecommerce leaders
  • Customer and partner experience teams
  • IT, architecture and product owners
  • Sales operations and enablement

If you’re involved in improving adoption, modernization, integration, or revenue through your portal, this session is designed for you.
