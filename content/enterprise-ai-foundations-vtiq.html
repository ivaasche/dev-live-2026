FAQ

What is Valtech IQ (VTIQ)?

Valtech IQ (VTIQ) is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that transforms trusted enterprise data into faster business decisions. It delivers real-time insights, recommendations and actions without requiring you to replace your existing systems.

How is VTIQ different from traditional BI or reporting tools?

Traditional BI tools tell you what happened. VTIQ helps you understand what to do next by turning trusted enterprise data into recommendations and actions.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. VTIQ connects to your existing ERP, CRM, manufacturing, supply chain and analytics platforms, allowing you to build on your current technology investments without a rip-and-replace approach.

Do we need an Enterprise AI Foundation first?

No. VTIQ can be deployed against focused business use cases using the data and systems you already have. For organizations looking to strengthen their AI capabilities over time, Enterprise AI Foundations provides a broader approach to connecting data, establishing governance and creating the foundation needed to scale AI across the enterprise. VTIQ is one of the ways organizations operationalize that foundation.

How quickly can we see value?

We start with one business function, one high-value decision and one measurable outcome. Most organizations begin demonstrating value within weeks before expanding across the enterprise.

Where does VTIQ create the most immediate value?

The highest-impact starting points are typically pricing and quoting, inventory visibility, demand forecasting, operational reporting and customer or distributor support — areas where trusted data already exists but decisions are still slowed by manual processes.
