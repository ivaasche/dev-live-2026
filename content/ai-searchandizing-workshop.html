A strategic 3–4-hour interactive workshop to assess and optimize your product data, search performance, and AI readiness—built for retailers, brands, and B2B companies looking to scale personalized commerce.

  • Executive Summary – Get a tailored action plan to improve discoverability, efficiency, and AI enablement.
