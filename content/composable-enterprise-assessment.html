Our Composable Enterprise Assessment helps organizations evaluate their ability to modularize business capabilities, enhance agility, and leverage digital transformation effectively.

By discussing your organization’s readiness and identifying a potential proof of concept, you can build a clear path forward.

What is it?

Our Composable Enterprise Assessment is a three-to-four-hour interactive session that quickly and effectively defines your MACH (microservices, API-first, cloud-native, headless) readiness, identifies a potential initiative, builds momentum in your organization and sets a clear path forward.

The workshop will help you quantify the value of composable architecture and prioritize your objectives so you can make a business decision about progressing with the initiative.

Why assess your readiness?

Composable technology improves the customer experience, introduces greater automation and makes your business more agile. The 2025 MACH Alliance Global Annual Researchfinds that nine in 10 organizations that have implemented composable technology say it has met or exceeded ROI.

It also led to an improved customer experience (for 55% of organizations), greater automation via improved systems and processes integration (54%) and greater organizational agility and/or adaptability to change (50%).

That’s not all. Enterprises that leverage composable architecture can:

  • Accelerate time to value. Reduce time-to-market for new products and services by 30-50% through modular business capabilities. Accelerate digital transformation initiatives without expensive full-scale replatforming.
  • Grow revenue. Enable hyper-personalization and new revenue streams by dynamically composing services for different customer segments. Improve cross-sell and upsell opportunities through data-driven, API-connected ecosystems.
  • Reduce costs. Save 15-25% on IT costs by decoupling legacy systems and adopting API-first, microservices-based architecture. Reduce technical debt and maintenance overhead by increasing software reusability.
  • Mitigate risk. Enable multi-cloud flexibility and minimize the risk of vendor lock-in. Improve compliance and security posture by segmenting critical business functions.
