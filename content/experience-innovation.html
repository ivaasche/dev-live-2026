Lexus Europe

Luxury made personal 

Lexus Europe had a web presence that worked wonderfully for a certain segment of consumers — people who love vehicle specs and configurators — but the experience was overwhelming for others. 

For those customers, we built an AI Concierge to answer their specific questions: “I have three kids. Will they fit comfortably in the back?” “Do my golf clubs fit in the boot?” “Can we fit a family of four, plus suitcases, plus our dog when we drive eight hours to our summer house?” 

The impact was immediate. Conversion rates nearly doubled, and completed vehicle configurations rose 41%.
