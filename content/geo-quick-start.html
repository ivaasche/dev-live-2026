Search has changed. Is your brand keeping up?

Visibility meant ranking on Google. Now, your customer can get an answer to their query without ever visiting your website. That means your brand is competing to get the attention of humans as well as an agentic audience. The competition moves from ranking and clicks to being included, represented accurately as well as being trusted. And that matters, because AI is the gatekeeper deciding which brands show up in front of the customers.

GEO QuickStart: know how AI sees you. Know what to change.

Starting with an intro call, GEO QuickStart is a focused 30-day Discovery and Quick Wins engagement that gives you a clear baseline of your AI visibility, identifies the gaps that matter most, and creates a practical roadmap for improving them.

After 30 days, you'll know:

  • Where your brand appears, where it doesn't and maps priority AI conversations.

  • How your AI visibility compares with key competitors, including where they are being mentioned or cited instead of you.

  • How AI engines describe your brand, products and services and where those answers are inaccurate, inconsistent or incomplete.

  • Which content, sources and authority signals are influencing those answers.

  • Whether your content and technical foundations are helping or preventing AI engines from finding, understanding and citing you.

  • Which changes will have greatest impact, with priority quick wins deployed within the engagement

  • Which KPIs to track and how to establish your baseline in Adobe Brand Visibility (an Adobe Product).

What you get:

  • A discovery workshop to define your priority audiences, markets, topics and GEO goals.
  • A managed prompt set in Adobe Brand Visibility (an Adobe product) to continuously track how AI describes and references your brand.
  • Onboarding and technical setup, so your team can start measuring and acting from day one.
  • A GEO diagnostic, competitive benchmark, prioritised recommendations and a practical roadmap for quick wins and next steps.
