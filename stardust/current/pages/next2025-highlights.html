Google Cloud Next 2025

Building what’s next: Innovation with Google Cloud and Valtech

Momentum is building. Explore what’s possible.

At Google Cloud Next 2025, Valtech showcased how Google Cloud’s industry-leading AI is helping businesses improve customer experience and enhance productivity with a series of new solutions.

Delivering experience innovation

  • Automotive: We’ve elevated the car-buying experience with a dynamic UI that adapts to conversational context, offering a valuable and non-intrusive opportunity to gain insights into customers.
  • Travel and Hospitality: Our solution lets customers book trips, manage inquiries and quickly access personalized assistance from travel agents.
  • Retail: We’ve developed several accelerators for AI Commerce Search, enabling brands to deliver higher quality search results.
  • Luxury: We’ve helped content and marketing teams extend their asset libraries exponentially. In seconds, they can add models, change backgrounds, and tweak motion, depth and dimension.
  • Media and entertainment: Our solution leverages Gen AI to efficiently extract features from large volumes of video, text and image content.
  • Financial services and insurance: We’ve showed how Gen AI can provide customer support and financial advisors with personalized insights, allowing more time to focus on building client relationships.

Let’s talk

Today's digital customers expect more. Personalised journeys, relevant product suggestions and seamless intelligent experiences. But many online platforms still feel static, confusing or disconnected. That's where Valtech's AI Concierge comes in. Transforming entire websites into adaptive intelligent assistants. Say a customer is shopping for a new car. They're expecting a baby and need more space. Instead of clicking through dozens of filters, they simply type or speak their needs into the site. AI Concierge understands the context, recommends a family-friendly vehicle and dynamically brings in information about things like interior features, stain-resistant materials and how it compares to the customer's current model. AI Concierge isn't a chatbot. It's fully integrated into the experience. It updates page content, configures products in real time and surfaces next best actions based on behaviour. All while maintaining traditional site functionality. AI Concierge can even switch gears when the customer changes their mind. Like from family vehicle to sports car. All in the same session without losing the journey context. Built using Google Cloud technologies like Vertex AI Search and Gemini, Valtech's AI Concierge leverages retrieval augmented generation and control generation to return dynamic components, not just static text. This means product pickers, image galleries and detailed answers appear as part of the process. a fluid, intelligent interface, not bolted on AI. With Valtech's AI Concierge, you're building smart, adaptive digital showrooms that convert better, engage deeper and exceed customer expectations.

Enabling advanced business productivity

We’re helping businesses unleash the productivity of their teams.

  • Content generation: Content and marketing teams can now do more in less time. In mere moments, they can add a model, try out backgrounds, and adjust motion, depth and dimension as needed for maximum impact. Models can be further customized by age, gender and skin tone for global applicability and cultural appropriateness.
  • Conversational analytics API: Empower your business users to ask data questions in plain language and get instant answers. With built-in Gen AI and NLP, this tool requires no coding and makes it easy to uncover insights for inventory planning, customer behavior analysis and quick decision-making. It integrates seamlessly with your existing data environment, enabling easy interactions with data, and Looker’s semantic layer improves response accuracy by minimizing hallucinations, ensuring trustworthy insights.
  • AI-assisted infrastructure migrations: Making a large-scale migration to Google Cloud? Together with CAST Software and mLogica, our accelerator leverages AI for assisted code generation. This increases the customer’s time to value in migrating workloads which lets your team focus on true innovation.
  • Commercetools deployment accelerators: Valtech has designed and delivered digital commerce platforms for international D2C and B2B brands. Leveraging this expertise, we offer solutions that accelerate your implementation of Commercetools.

Unlocking new possibilities with Google Cloud

These solutions unite Valtech’s ability to identify pain points and opportunities with Google Cloud’s latest advances.

  • Gemini Enterprise: A unified platform to leverage Google's advanced AI and ML capabilities.
  • Gemini: Google's most advanced and largest AI model is a versatile tool and personal assistant. You can access Gemini through Agent Platform.
  • Enterprise AI for business users: Agentspace lets your business users easily locate information, make faster decisions and automate tasks and workflows. It seamlessly integrates with your existing systems, combining a Google-quality search engine with multi-modal search capabilities enhanced by AI agents and Gemini’s generative reasoning.

These advances are built with security in mind. Your business maintains complete control over your data, using existing permissions and access controls to ensure employees only see content they are authorized to access.
